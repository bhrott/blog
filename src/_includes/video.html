The title is not decoration — it becomes the iframe's accessible name, and an iframe without one is announced to a screen reader as nothing more useful than "frame". Two choices worth keeping: * youtube-nocookie.com, so a reader who scrolls past without pressing play is not handed an ad-profiling cookie by a page they only read. * loading="lazy", because the player pulls several hundred KB of script. Below the fold it costs nothing until the reader gets there. The aspect ratio and the chalk frame live in .video-embed (assets/main.scss); nothing here sets width or height, so the box follows the column on a phone. {%- endcomment -%}
