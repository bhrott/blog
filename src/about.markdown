I'm Ben-Hur Santos Ott. I write about application security, offensive tooling,
and building software with AI agents you can actually trust.

This site is field notes, not tutorials. Most posts start as something I had to
work out during an assessment or while shipping code, written down so I stop
re-deriving it.

## What you'll find here

- **[Application security](/tags/web/)** — how the OWASP Top 10 shows up in real
  pentests, [JWT attacks](/tags/jwt/), and the
  [secrets-management failures](/tags/secrets/) in nearly every codebase.
- **[Offensive tooling and recon](/tags/recon/)** — [Nmap](/tags/nmap/),
  [Burp Suite](/tags/burp/), subdomain takeovers, and
  [Linux privilege escalation](/tags/privesc/).
- **[Defense and detection](/tags/blue-team/)** —
  [SSH hardening](/tags/ssh/), [YARA rules](/tags/yara/), and the
  [threat modeling](/tags/threat-modeling/) pass that fits in twenty minutes.

Everything is filed by [tag](/tags/), and there's an
[RSS feed](/feed.xml) if you'd rather not remember to come back.
