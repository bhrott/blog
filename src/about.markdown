## whoami

I'm Ben-Hur Santos Ott. I write about software security (aka AppSec, DevSecOps, etc), and the
awkward new problem of securing systems that take instructions from strangers
and act on them, we like to call them "AI Agents".

This site is field notes, not tutorials. Most posts start as something I had to
work out during an assessment or while shipping code, written down so I stop
re-deriving it.

Everything is filed by [tag](/tags/), and there's an
[RSS feed](/feed.xml) if you'd rather not remember to come back.
